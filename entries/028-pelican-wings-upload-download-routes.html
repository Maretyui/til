Pelican's file upload/download aren't under /api/servers like everything else

After putting a reverse proxy in front of Pelican Panel + Wings, the console, power actions, and stats all worked fine through a single forwarded location for /api/servers — but the Files tab's upload and download buttons both just 404'd, with nothing obviously wrong in the panel's own config.

Wings has its own separate top-level routes for file transfer

Everything else the browser needs from Wings directly (console websocket, live resource stats) really does live under /api/servers/<uuid>/..., which made it reasonable to assume one proxy location covering that prefix would be enough. It isn't — file upload and download use their own separate top-level paths instead: 

/upload/file
/download/file
/download/backup

Those never match a /api/servers prefix rule, so without their own explicit proxy locations pointing at Wings, they just 404 at the reverse proxy before ever reaching the daemon.

The fix

Add dedicated proxy locations for /upload and /download (not just /api/servers) pointed at the same Wings backend. If Wings ever adds more browser-facing endpoints, checking its own binary for other top-level route prefixes settles it faster than trial and error: 

strings /usr/local/bin/wings | grep -iE '"/upload|/download'

The actual lesson

Don't assume a single API-prefix proxy rule covers everything a daemon exposes to the browser just because most of it does. Treat "the console works" and "file transfer works" as two separate things to verify — they can hit completely different route prefixes on the same backend.
