Scrolling to the bottom of a modal list kept scrolling the page underneath

A mobile-safe modal with a scrollable list inside it had an annoying bug: flinging a finger to scroll to the bottom of the list, once the list ran out of content to scroll, handed the rest of that swipe's momentum to the page behind the modal — the background jumped, even though the modal itself has position: fixed and the body was visually locked.

Why: scroll chaining is the default, and fixed positioning doesn't stop it

When a scrollable element hits the end of its own scroll range, the browser's default behavior is to let the scroll "chain" to the next scrollable ancestor and keep consuming the gesture there. That's independent of layout — it happens purely because the touch/wheel input has leftover delta once the inner element can't move any further, and overflow: hidden or position: fixed on the body doesn't change how touch scroll chaining is resolved. 

.modal-list {
  overflow-y: auto;
  overscroll-behavior: contain;
}

The actual lesson

overscroll-behavior: contain on the scrollable element stops the chaining at its own boundary — overscrolling past the top or bottom still shows the native bounce/glow effect locally, it just never reaches the ancestor. The stronger overscroll-behavior: none suppresses that local effect too. Either is simpler and more reliable than the older workaround of a touchmove listener that calls preventDefault(), which also has to special-case being at the exact top/bottom to avoid breaking scrolling inside the element itself.
